# TASC-Virtual-Chase-App
A storm chase simulator in your browser. Replay real severe weather days on live NEXRAD radar and drive real roads against the clock.


Virtual Chase is a storm chase simulator that runs entirely in your browser, from a single file with nothing to install. Pick any severe weather day since 2011, or chase live. It draws real NEXRAD Level II radar itself: reflectivity, velocity, storm-relative velocity, echo tops and the dual-pol products. You drive real roads that obey posted speed limits, stop at lights and stop signs, and slow down for corners. The radar updates every five minutes and the clock keeps running between scans, so you’re always driving on a picture that’s a few minutes old, just like the real thing.


Radar: the national IEM composite, or full-resolution NOAA Level II for any site, archived or live. You can run two radar products side by side in split view and load your own GR2Analyst / GRLevel3 color tables.
Driving: real road routing with posted speed limits, traffic lights, stop signs, train crossings and corner slowdowns. You can choose between alternate routes and re-route in the middle of a leg.
Storm awareness: NWS warning polygons, SPC storm reports, storm tracks, and storm motion measured from the radar itself. Map views show how far you can get in a given time and which roads will keep you clear of the core.
Chasing together: up to eight color-coded chasers, plus a no-peek mode that won’t let you look ahead on the radar.
Export: GRLevelX placefiles, plus GPX, KML, CSV and GeoJSON tracks, and save files so you can reload a chase later.
