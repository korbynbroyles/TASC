# TASC-Virtual-Chase-App
A storm chase simulator in your browser. Replay real severe weather days on live NEXRAD radar and drive real roads against the clock.


Virtual Chase is a storm chase simulator that runs entirely in your browser, from a single file with nothing to install. Pick any severe weather day since 2011, or chase live. It draws real NEXRAD Level II radar itself: reflectivity, velocity, storm-relative velocity, echo tops and the dual-pol products. You drive real roads that obey posted speed limits, stop at lights and stop signs, and slow down for corners. The radar updates every five minutes and the clock keeps running between scans, so you’re always driving on a picture that’s a few minutes old, just like the real thing.
